# Motion rules (all styles)

Lessons from real client feedback. Follow every one.

## Layout
- One focus per moment: the product, the character, or the words. Never stack a full-bleed photo under a text band: it looks like a slideshow.
- Photos are **small framed cards** (white frame, soft shadow, slight tilt) or **clean cut-outs** floating on the background. Not full-screen backgrounds.
- The product is never huge: about 35–45% of the frame height. No giant label close-ups.
- Text never covers the product, a face, a cup or the thing being talked about. Keep text in the top third; keep the character in a bottom corner and never put text behind them.
- Keep a 60 px safe margin on every side (phone UI covers the edges).
- Chips/pills with coloured backgrounds behind words (green, red…) look cheap. Use coloured *text* in the brand palette instead.

## Text
- Kinetic typography word by word, timed to the voice (fade + rise + slight blur-in).
- Arabic: right-to-left, never animate letter by letter, letters must stay connected in every frame. Use a font that shapes Arabic fully (Cairo is safe; some calligraphic fonts break words like «المزاج»). Check every Arabic word at full size.
- Minimum size on a 1080-wide vertical frame: 70 px for lines, 150 px+ for the hero word.
- Brand/product names on screen in their own spelling (Latin names stay Latin).

## Motion
- Easing: ease-out cubic for entrances, gentle overshoot (back easing ≤1.4) for pops, ease-in-out for camera moves.
- Something always moves slowly (float, drift, slow zoom); nothing is perfectly still.
- Cuts on the beat or on a spoken word; optional 0.1 s light flash or soft fade on cuts. No wipes.
- Elements that stay across shots (a character, a logo) must not re-enter on every cut: animate them in once.

## Characters
- Keep one character design for the whole ad; use 3–4 expression variants of the same drawing (skeptical, shocked, smiling, eyes-closed smelling…).
- Lip-sync from audio amplitude usually looks fast and wrong. Default: no lip movement, change expressions with the lines instead. Only try lip-sync if the user asks, and show a test first.

## Sound
- No music unless asked. Soft effects only: whoosh on cuts, pop on word hits, low thump on the key word, chime on the brand name, glass shimmer on light sweeps.
- Effects about -20 to -24 LUFS under a -16 LUFS voice; no hiss, no harsh clicks.
